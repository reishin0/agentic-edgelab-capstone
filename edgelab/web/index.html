How to read ETF entry costs

Match the time
On each sampled morning, the model uses the same second in New York for all three ETFs. It picks the first second when each full purchase fits the size limits in both scenarios.
Read costs in dollars and basis points
One basis point (bp) is 0.01%. The quoted spread is the gap between the bid and ask. The study measures purchase cost against the midpoint value of the whole shares purchased.
Compare base and stress
Base uses the quoted ask, 1 bp of adverse slippage and a $1 commission. Stress doubles the distance from midpoint to ask and uses 5 bp of slippage with the same commission.
Check the scope
These are simulated purchase costs for the saved mornings. The study measures entry costs only.
