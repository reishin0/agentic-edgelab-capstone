The separate-walk point estimate cleared the 2% ceiling, but its upper
confidence bound missed. For the correlated walks, 37 trials reached
stability and the bootstrap advanced 28. In the weak-spread late-break
case, 775 reached stability and the bootstrap advanced 467. The paired
540-observation CUSUM advanced zero trials in either case. This exposes
where power gains for stable spreads came with an unacceptable increase
in false advancement. Timing also matters for the shorter comparator.
Breaks after its last observation are outside its information set.
